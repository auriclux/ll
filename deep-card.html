The Key

What you are looking at

At first glance, an ordinary Christmas card. Look closer. Twenty small doors stand open in it — in ornaments, presents, the clock, the branches, even the wrapping paper — and every door opens on a real place, a real moment, or a world that ought to be real. This is the key to them.

And one thing that is not numbered: look out of the window, into the snow, a moment longer than you meant to.
